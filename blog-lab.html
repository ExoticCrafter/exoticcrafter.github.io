Lab - PC Assembly

30 October, 2025 | Video Editing

Description

On October 30, 2025, our Technology and Information System class held a hands-on PC Assembly practical session at N28. Accompanied by Dr. Aryati and technical staff, my team—Farah Aisya, Nurain Nabilah, Humayra' and Shasya Shafieqah—worked together to disassemble and reassemble a computer.

This activity is a crucial component of our e-portfolio, showcasing our technical progression and understanding of hardware artifacts. By identifying parts like the CPU cooler, RAM, and PSU, we translated theoretical knowledge into physical practice to meet the core course requirements.

Reflection

What I Gained

I gained practical, hands-on experience in identifying and handling sensitive hardware components such as RAM, CPU coolers, and Power Supply Units. Through the disassembly and assembly process, I learned the correct sequence of unplugging connectors and unscrewing parts to ensure the machine remains functional. Additionally, the post-session quiz helped reinforce my understanding of hardware architecture and component roles. Working with my team also improved my ability to collaborate on technical tasks, ensuring everyone contributed to the final working PC

Areas for Improvement

I realized that I need to improve my speed and confidence when identifying specific motherboard power connectors and slots to ensure a more efficient assembly process. I also aim to refine my technical vocabulary so I can explain the function of each component more professionally during team discussions. Furthermore, I want to become more proficient at troubleshooting hardware issues that might arise during the initial boot-up phase after assembly. Strengthening these areas will help me reach the "Excellent" standard for content development in future technical reports.

Final Thoughts

This session was a highlight of the semester because it provided a tangible connection to the hardware concepts described in the SECP1513 syllabus. The assistance from the staff and Dr. Aryati made the learning environment supportive and sparked a greater interest in computer architecture. I feel that this practical experience is essential for any Data Engineering student to understand the physical infrastructure that supports digital systems. Ultimately, the excitement of successfully booting the PC we assembled ourselves was a rewarding conclusion to an insightful day.
